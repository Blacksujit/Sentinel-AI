# Trust Score

SentinelAI condenses six parallel detector signals into a single, explainable number: the **trust score**, from 0 to 100.

## The scale

| Band | Range | Meaning | Action |
|------|-------|---------|--------|
| **Trusted** | 0–24 | No material risk detected | Serve as-is |
| **Needs review** | 25–59 | Some signals flagged, output questionable | Auto-correct or flag for humans |
| **Hallucinated** | 60–100 | High-confidence hallucination or attack | Block and escalate |

## Why it's not a black box

Every score ships with **reasons** — the specific tokens and signals that drove the number.

```json
{
  "trust_score": 72,
  "status": "hallucinated",
  "reasons": [
    {
      "detector": "fabricated_citation",
      "confidence": 0.94,
      "evidence": "Citation [1] references a DOI that does not exist",
      "tokens": ["IEEE", "978-1-7281-3277-8"]
    },
    {
      "detector": "overconfidence",
      "confidence": 0.81,
      "evidence": "Absolute claims made without supporting context"
    }
  ]
}
```

## How to use it

- **Debug in seconds, not hours** — the reasons tell you exactly why a response was flagged
- **Tune your policy** — choose the threshold where your app corrects vs. blocks
- **Audit with confidence** — every score has provenance your compliance team can follow

<Callout type="warning">
  The score is a risk indicator, not a guarantee. SentinelAI flags risk
  honestly — it never claims to guarantee safety or alignment.
</Callout>
